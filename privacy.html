Privacy Policy

Omar Osama Ali | Cairo, Egypt

This page explains what FLAGVERSE stores and what it does not collect.

Information stored on your device

FLAGVERSE uses browser local storage for game settings, player names entered for local play, saved focus sets, and game progress. This information stays in the browser unless you clear the site data or remove it through the browser controls.

Information sent to third parties

The app does not use an account system, advertising SDK, analytics service, or social media embed. Country flag images are requested from the flag image service used by the game when a flag is displayed. Your browser may send the normal technical request information required to retrieve those images.

Contact

Project owner: Omar Osama Ali. Location: Cairo, Egypt. Email: omar-osama2011@yahoo.com.

Your choices

You can clear local game data from the Settings screen or through your browser's site-data controls. Do not enter sensitive personal information into player-name fields.
