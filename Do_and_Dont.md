# LOCAL CHESS

## Design, UX, Accessibility and Quality Rules

**Purpose:** This document defines the strict **DO** and **DON'T** rules
for the Local 2-Player Chess website.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# DO

## DESIGN

-   Use black, charcoal, white, and deep green.
-   Optional restrained gold accents are allowed.
-   Keep the design premium and minimal.
-   Make the chessboard the visual priority.
-   Use strong color contrast.
-   Use clear typography.
-   Use clean rectangular buttons with modest corner radius.
-   Keep controls visually organized.
-   Make the interface responsive.
-   Make the game work well on mobile first.

## GAMEPLAY

-   Make it genuinely local.
-   Two players use the same device.
-   No account is required.
-   Make the game work without online multiplayer.
-   Implement real chess rules.
-   Show legal moves.
-   Show the current player's turn.
-   Highlight the last move.
-   Clearly indicate Check.
-   Clearly indicate Checkmate.
-   Support Castling.
-   Support En Passant.
-   Support Promotion.
-   Support Draw conditions.
-   Support Undo.
-   Support Restart.
-   Support Rematch.
-   Support Resign.
-   Track actual player wins.
-   Track actual draws.
-   Clearly display the winner.

## UX

-   Keep the flow simple.
-   Make Player Setup obvious.
-   Make Start Game obvious.
-   Make Rematch easy.
-   Keep secondary features inside a Menu.
-   Support Auto Flip.
-   Support manual Flip.
-   Keep the board large enough to use comfortably.
-   Avoid gameplay scrolling on mobile.
-   Use subtle animations only where they improve feedback.

## ACCESSIBILITY

-   Check color contrast.
-   Use clear button labels.
-   Use semantic HTML.
-   Provide keyboard-friendly forms.
-   Provide visible focus states.
-   Do not rely only on color.
-   Use meaningful alt text for meaningful images.
-   Make form labels explicit.
-   Keep touch targets usable.

## PRIVACY

-   Collect only necessary data.
-   Avoid unnecessary personal information.
-   Check tracking.
-   Check cookies.
-   Check third-party embeds.
-   Use form consent when applicable.
-   Check applicable local laws.
-   Check copyright and licensing.

## CONTENT

-   Use precise interface copy.
-   Avoid unsupported claims.
-   Keep the creator credit subtle.

## BRANDING

**Made by Omar Osama**

**https://omar-osama-ali.github.io/**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# DON'T

## VISUAL

-   Do not use purple.
-   Do not use purple gradients.
-   Do not use purple accents.
-   Do not use excessive gradients.
-   Do not create visual clutter.
-   Do not overuse glass effects.
-   Do not use excessive animations.
-   Do not use pill-shaped buttons.
-   Do not use emoji icons as the main icon system.
-   Do not use cursor animations.
-   Do not use fake visual effects just to appear impressive.

## CONTENT

-   Do not use vague hero text.
-   Do not use AI-sounding marketing copy.
-   Do not use unsupported claims.
-   Do not use fake reviews.
-   Do not use fake testimonials.
-   Do not use fake metrics.
-   Do not use fake counters.

## TECHNICAL

-   Do not add online multiplayer.
-   Do not add login.
-   Do not add signup.
-   Do not add matchmaking.
-   Do not add unnecessary backend functionality.
-   Do not add an AI opponent.
-   Do not collect unnecessary user data.
-   Do not add unnecessary tracking.

## AI / BRANDING

-   Do not use AI-generated decorative images.
-   Do not add a "Made with AI" tag.
-   Do not make the website look like an AI-generated template.

## ANIMATION

-   Do not use excessive scroll animations.
-   Do not animate everything.
-   Do not sacrifice performance for visual effects.

## LEGAL

-   Do not make unsupported legal or compliance claims.
-   Do not use copyrighted chess pieces, sounds, fonts, images, or other
    assets without appropriate rights.
-   Do not add unnecessary legal pages solely to make the website appear
    more professional.
